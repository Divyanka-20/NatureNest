Indian Tigers

The Bengal tiger, the largest tiger subspecies, roams the forests and grasslands of India. Known for its majestic appearance and powerful build, the tiger is a symbol of strength and grace in Indian wildlife.

Indian Elephants

Indian elephants, slightly smaller than their African cousins, are known for their social structure and intelligence. They play a crucial role in maintaining the ecosystem by dispersing seeds and creating water holes.

Indian Rhinos

With their distinctive single horn, Indian rhinos are primarily found in the grasslands of northeastern India. Conservation efforts have helped increase their numbers, but they remain a vulnerable species.
