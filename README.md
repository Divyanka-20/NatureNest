## Overview🐅

NatureNest is an informational web platform dedicated to showcasing the rich biodiversity of India. The project highlights Indian forests, national parks, wildlife species, habitats, and conservation-related information through a clean, user-friendly interface.
It aims to promote awareness, education, and appreciation of India’s natural heritage.

---

## Features🌳 

   - Explore Indian Forests & Biodiversity
   - Detailed information on:
      - National Parks
      - Reserve Forests
      - Wildlife Sanctuaries
      - Animal Species
      - Bird Species
      - Endangered Species
      - Additional details on:
      - Forest Bungalows
      - Nearby Hotels
      - Forest Tour Plans
   - Categorized and easy-to-navigate content
   - Educational and awareness-focused design
